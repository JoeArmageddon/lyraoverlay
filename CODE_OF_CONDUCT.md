# Code of conduct

Lyra is a small, friendly project. Everyone who takes part (users reporting bugs, people sending code,
fonts or themes, and maintainers) is expected to make it a pleasant place to work.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree about the work, not the person.
- Keep feedback specific and useful. "This overlaps the menu bar on a notched display, here is a
  screenshot" beats "this is broken".
- Respect people's time, skill level and language.
- Share only things you have the right to share: your own code, fonts, themes and images, or ones whose
  license allows it, and never other people's private information.

## What we do not accept

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks, publishing someone's private
information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block
people who do so repeatedly. To report a problem, use the private reporting feature described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (choose "Report a vulnerability" and say it is a conduct report) or contact
a maintainer directly. Reports are handled privately.

This code of conduct applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions.
