# Lyra Gothic

Single-weight Regular display typeface for large on-screen lyrics (Lyra app). Original design, generated procedurally by `build_gothic.py` (with shared `fontkit.py`); no existing typeface was traced or derived.

Files: `LyraGothic-Regular.ttf`, `specimen.png`, `build_gothic.py`, `fontkit.py`.
Rebuild: `/tmp/fontvenv/bin/python build_gothic.py` (needs fontTools, skia-pathops, numpy, pillow).

## Design spec
Modern Textura-spirit blackletter. Pen model: a broad rectangular nib (106 x 12 units) held at 37 degrees, swept along angular skeleton paths (hull of successive nib positions, unioned with skia-pathops). Verticals are heavy (about 90 units), 35-degree strokes are hairlines, shoulders are chamfered ("broken") rather than round, stems end in diamond feet and hairline diamond heads, high x-height (500) over cap height 700, tight vertical rhythm. Lowercase share one construction: octagonal bowl plus stem (a b d g o p q), n-arch family (h m n r u). Capitals are plain, angular and unambiguous (A E G H M S T U V W built as large, open forms). Digits are lining, narrow; 0 is narrower than O.

## Technical
- unitsPerEm 1000; ascender 800, descender -200, capHeight 700, xHeight 500, lineGap 0; OS/2 winAscent 900 / winDescent 250, USE_TYPO_METRICS set (hhea 800/-200/0).
- TrueType quadratic container (glyf), outer contours clockwise, overlaps removed by boolean union; GPOS `kern` generated by an optical auto-kerner (profile-gap based, with a minimum-gap guard). Names: family "Lyra Gothic", style Regular, PostScript "LyraGothic-Regular".
- Covers A-Z a-z 0-9, space, the full requested punctuation set and Latin accents (accented glyphs are base letter + mark).
- Automated check (all required characters present, no empty glyphs except space, no overlaps, correct winding): passed.

## Known weak spots
- Blackletter character is simplified: no true double-storey a/g, no ligatures or long s; it reads as an angular condensed face with blackletter details more than a historical Textura.
- Hairline accents and diamond heads are used sparingly; capitals are not richly decorated.
- l, k and f ascender heads are slightly awkward (diamond cap looks pinched at small sizes); ß is a plain construction.
- Accents (Ç, ñ, ä...) use one shared mark set; tilde and ring are tiny at 28 pt.
- Curves are polylines (fine segments), not true quadratic splines; extrema are approximated by dense points.
- Not tested in Font Book/Core Text/Safari in this environment (checked with fontTools and Pillow/FreeType only); no TrueType hinting.
- The specimen is drawn glyph by glyph from the .ttf advances and GPOS kern values.
