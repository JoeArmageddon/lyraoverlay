# Lyra Thorn Drawn

Files: `LyraThornDrawn-Regular.ttf`, `build_thorn.py` (generator; also needs `lyra_fontkit.py`, `nibkit.py`), `specimen.png`.
Rebuild: `/tmp/fontvenv/bin/python build_thorn.py` (needs fontTools, skia-pathops, numpy, Pillow).

Family "Lyra Thorn Drawn", style Regular, PostScript "LyraThornDrawn-Regular". 1000 upm, hhea/typo ascender 800, descender -200, lineGap 0, winAscent 900,
winDescent 250, USE_TYPO_METRICS set, capHeight 700. Quadratic TrueType outlines, merged contours, outer contours clockwise.
Covers A-Z a-z 0-9, the required punctuation and Latin accents (see brief), plus NBSP and dotless i.
All outlines are original, generated by code; nothing traced or derived from any typeface.

## Design spec
Gothic barbed capitals. A broad-nib pen is simulated: stroke width follows direction (stems 84 units thick, horizontals 32,
nib angle 12 degrees so '\\' diagonals are heavier than '/'), strokes taper to thorn points, and barbs are tapered curved spikes
growing from stems, bar ends and terminals. Ribbons are merged with skia-pathops, refitted to smooth cubics (Schneider fit,
tolerance ~2 units, corners kept sharp) and converted to quadratics.

- Proportions: tall and narrow caps (700). Lowercase is a small-cap style at 78% height (546; OS/2 sxHeight = 546, not the 500
  suggested in the brief), built from the same shapes at reduced size with the same stroke weights so it stays readable.
- Numerals: tall and thin, with small barbs and flags.
- Kerning: automatic pair kerning (GPOS kern, ~9000 pairs incl. accented variants) from rendered ink profiles.
- Mood: dark romance, ironwork, vines.

## Known weak spots (honest list)
- Lowercase is small caps, so b/d/p/q-type ascender/descender shapes do not exist; descenders in g j p q y are absent.
- Barbs are small (about 100 units) and can fill in at 28 px; hairlines (32 units) are thin for 28 px on low-DPI screens.
- Dots, commas, quotes and semicolons are fine tapered spikes and look faint next to the letters; ß is close to B.
- Some bar-end barbs and tapered joins (e.g. U, M, N, K) are slightly crude where tapers meet other strokes.
- Outlines come from curve fitting; a few glyphs have more points than a hand-drawn font and curve extrema are only approximate.
- Not tested in Font Book/Core Text here; verified with fontTools and Pillow/FreeType only.
