# Lyra Neon Drawn

Files: `LyraNeonDrawn-Regular.ttf`, `build_neon.py` (generator; also needs `lyra_fontkit.py`), `specimen.png`.
Rebuild: `/tmp/fontvenv/bin/python build_neon.py` (needs fontTools, skia-pathops, numpy, Pillow).

Family "Lyra Neon Drawn", style Regular, PostScript "LyraNeonDrawn-Regular". 1000 upm, hhea/typo ascender 800, descender -200, lineGap 0, winAscent 900,
winDescent 250, USE_TYPO_METRICS set, capHeight 700. Quadratic TrueType outlines, merged contours, outer contours clockwise.
Covers A-Z a-z 0-9, the required punctuation and Latin accents (see brief), plus NBSP and dotless i.
All outlines are original, generated by code; nothing traced or derived from any typeface.

## Design spec
Bent-glass neon tube signage. Every glyph is a set of centre-line strokes (lines, elliptical arcs, rounded polylines)
stroked with one constant-width (64 units) round-capped, round-joined pen via skia-pathops, merged by boolean union, and
written as quadratic TrueType outlines.

- Weight/contrast: monoline, 64-unit tube, no contrast. Caps 700 tall (ink), x-height 500, ascenders ~720, descender ~ -200.
- Shapes: fully rounded terminals and corners, ellipse-based bowls, single-storey a and g, tailed l, serifed capital I,
  dotted-less slashed 0 (narrow oval with inner slash) vs wide round O.
- Spacing: generous side bearings (60-80 units) so a glow does not merge letters; automatic pair kerning (GPOS kern,
  ~5000 pairs incl. accented variants) measured from rendered ink profiles.
- Mood: bar sign at night, retro diner.

## Known weak spots (honest list)
- No literal 'tube passes behind itself' gaps except the X/x crossings, which have a small break in one stroke; no electrode caps.
- Accent marks are drawn with the same tube, so rings (Å, å) have a very small counter and read as blobs at 28 px.
- & @ % $ ß are simple constructions and the least refined glyphs; the middle of 3 is a cusp of the tube.
- Ascender glyph tops (b d h k l) sit slightly above cap height by design.
- Auto-kerning is geometric, not hand-tuned; a few pairs (e.g. 'T' + lowercase) may look slightly loose or tight.
- Not tested in Font Book/Core Text here; verified with fontTools and Pillow/FreeType only.
